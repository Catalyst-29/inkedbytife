How it Works

The Ink Journey

01

Consultation

Fill out our digital request form. We review your concept, size, references, and ideal placement, aligning on your creative direction.

02

Design Concept

Tife designs a custom artwork outline tailored precisely to your anatomy, sending a digital sketch mock-up for comments and approval.

03

The Session

In a private, clean, and comfortable studio room, we size and stencil the design. Using precision needles, we craft your final art piece.

04

Aftercare

We supply specialized second-skin healing wrap and detailed guidelines. We are here to support your skin's healing process continuously.
